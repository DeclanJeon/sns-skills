# YouTube Growth Playbook

**Market research → demand check → entry point, pattern and structure → test.** For new growth opportunities, audience demand chooses what is worth testing; the channel determines how to deliver it. Existing genres, the creator's preferences, and the channel's biggest upload are not the default search scope.

## Route the request

- **Market entry (default for growth strategy, starting a channel, or choosing the next concept):** Read [market entry](references/market-entry.md) and [Million Index API](references/million-index.md). Fix only the decision objective and actual scope limits before searching (e.g. geography, language, time, hard exclusions). If there is no keyword, begin with discovery instead of requiring a channel niche or premise. Use `topic-demand-selection` where available for the demand decision; Million Index is the first retrieval source. If the user restricts the market explicitly, honor that restriction.
- **Existing-video diagnosis or operating cadence:** Use the [operating guide](references/유튜브-성장-운영-지침서.md), especially its metric and decision tables. Use first-party data for diagnosis and baselines. Do not turn a low CTR, view total, or subscriber total into a cause without comparable data. If the user also asks what opportunity to pursue next, use market entry for that decision.
- **Research only:** Stop at evidence and demand assessment. **Production requested:** hand off the approved concept to the applicable production workflow. This skill does not authorize account access, paid tooling, posting or media reuse.

## Deliver a decision

For market entry, return: **demand verdict and evidence → entry point and reusable pattern → one original concept and format → test and decision rule**. A market pattern describes what viewers appear to want, not a guaranteed algorithm trigger. Mark observations, hypotheses and unknowns. If evidence is thin, give a provisional test or withhold the recommendation; say what could change the verdict. Expected views are optional and require comparable first-party data, not competitors' hits.

For diagnosis, return the evidence-backed bottleneck or exact missing metric and the next one-variable test. Keep Shorts metrics (watch choice, engaged views, average percentage) separate from long-form metrics (traffic-source impressions/CTR, early retention). Compare the same upload age and metric definitions. Neither format has a universal 60%/40-second cutoff.
